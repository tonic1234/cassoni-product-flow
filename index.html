Conceived as glamour furniture for residential projects and hospitality lounges, the piece sits naturally in contemporary living rooms as well as in more classic settings, where a measured decorative accent is required. Its presence is discreet yet distinct, and the combination of drawn metal and soft padding keeps the volume balanced from every angle.

Floyd Nightstand by Rugiano

Crafted by the masters of high-end Italian glamour at Rugiano, the Floyd Nightstand is an elite bedside masterpiece, perfectly suited for palatial master bedrooms, upscale penthouse suites, and luxury boutique hotel chambers. The piece is defined by a pure, compact cylindrical volume entirely clad in premium leather with fine visible stitching that highlights its round silhouette. It reflects a luxury lifestyle that delights in multi-tier architectural detail; a dramatic upper marble top acts as a canvas for striking organic veining, while the open understructure reveals a calibrated interplay of solids and voids featuring a matching second marble shelf—all bound together by a precision bronzed metal framework that integrates a handle-free, seamless storage drawer. Made in Italy.

A Cylindrical Nightstand in Leather and Stone

The composition is deliberately quiet: the round volume reads as a sculptural presence, a softer counterpoint to the linear geometries that surround it. What distinguishes the piece is the dialogue of materials — the warmth of the hide against the cool veining of the stone, mediated by the dry lustre of the bronzed metal, with every transition resolved through the quiet precision of Italian craftsmanship. The curve invites the hand as much as the eye, and glamour is entrusted to proportion and finish rather than applied ornament. Within the New RUGIANO Collection, this leather bedside table stands as a study in restraint.
